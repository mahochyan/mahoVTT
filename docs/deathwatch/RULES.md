# Rules profile and limits

Baseline: Deathwatch Core Rulebook (2010), with individually applied Living Errata
v1.1 corrections. IDs pin the profile to `deathwatch-core-2010-errata1.1/preview1`.
This is a compatibility preview, not a certification of complete official coverage.
The rule text, illustrations and complete stat block catalogue are not redistributed.
Book page numbers below are printed pages unless marked PDF.

## Implemented, with automatic checks

- D100 roll-under; complete tens determine Degrees, without an automatic +1.
  Combined test modifiers cap at +/-60. Attack rolls 01 and 96+ receive their
  special handling; Focus Power rolls 91+ fail. Core 202–205,244.
- Six core Chapters and Specialties, Chapter exclusions, 2d10+30 or 100-point
  allocation, one optional initial characteristic reroll, Wounds and Fate rolls.
  Initial XP: 12,000 spent and 1,000 available. Characteristic advances supported;
  this is NOT a complete purchase catalogue of skills/talents/implants. Core 25–91.
- 1d10+Ag Bonus initiative, two half actions or a full action, attack/concentration
  subtype limits, Aim, Ready, Reload, jam clearance, standard/semiauto/fullauto,
  called shot, All Out, Guarded, Charge, Swift Attack, defensive stance.
  Original Deathwatch burst bonuses: +10/+20, not later game variants. Core 235–244.
- Server-owned rolls, player Fate confirmation before committing outcomes,
  reaction selection and optional failed-reaction reroll, hit-location reversal,
  additional-hit table, per-location armour, Pen, Toughness, Tearing, Righteous
  Fury and True Grit. Errata RF uses exploding d10, not a second weapon roll.
  Reaching zero remaining Wounds does NOT automatically kill/incapacitate.
- Critical damage is accumulated, then the game stops at an explicit human ruling
  with damage type/location/value/book reference. Complete Critical tables and
  continuing injury effects are not automatically encoded. Core 250–259.
- Finite magazine/reserve, supported requisition cost/Renown checks; explicit
  core vs optional revised bolter profiles. The default is core weapon data.
- Horde Magnitude, size, weapon hits, explosive extra loss, selected Specialty
  damage, morale and group attack. Full rules for all Horde traits are not covered.
- Oath and leader selection, base Cohesion, support distance, Solo/Squad entry,
  Tactical Spacing reaction transfer; Strongpoint target bonus only. Other squad
  abilities retain a choice, expenditure/referral and manual ruling, not complete
  automatic multi-actor effects. No claim of all Chapter squad/solo abilities.
- First Aid outside combat, per-injury treatment guard and Apothecary modifiers.
  Long-term medical care, death and recovery between sessions require adjudication.
- Smite with range/PR damage, affected allies, area Dodge relocation; Fettered /
  Unfettered / Push selection. Phenomena and Perils stop for manual table lookup.
  Other listed powers are selectable but manually adjudicated. Sustaining powers
  and advanced psychic interactions are not fully automated.

## Explicit adapter conventions and known gaps

1. One square is one metre. Eight-direction grid movement charges one metre per
   traversed square; range uses geometric distance. Corner cutting is blocked.
   A map is a tactical slice, not an exact reconstruction of the book's full scene.
2. Where the additional-hit table says Arm without specifying side, preserve the
   original arm side, otherwise use the right. This is an adapter convention.
3. Starting ammunition is a declared mission allocation: normal weapons have
   three spare magazines; the Devastator starts with a 250-round backpack supply.
   Arbitrary ammo types and an exhaustive supply economy are absent.
4. Only a subset of the Marine's trained skills, talents and implants has active
   mechanics. Quick Draw, Unnatural Strength/Toughness and power-armour bonuses
   are encoded; Chapter signature effects are usually references/manual controls.
5. Cover protection applies by occupied cover cell/location. Destructible cover,
   all angle/stance effects, precise visibility distance and all lighting penalties
   are not fully simulated. There is no complete fog-of-war exploration map.
6. Movement combined with bursts, Overwatch, Suppressive Fire, grappling, vehicles,
   jump-pack manoeuvres, multiple separately selected burst targets, every Horde
   special rule and all expansion careers need additional implementations.
7. Horde secondary targets currently reuse the initiating attack modifier bundle;
   adjudicate target-specific modifiers where necessary. This is flagged partial.
8. Medical tools and jump packs are Specialty capabilities/references, not yet a
   full inventory with every armour history and gear effect.
9. Fear: the Solo-mode Marine Willpower penalty is automatic; Squad-mode Cohesion
   damage and unusual Fear outcomes require the human ruling interface.
10. Fate regeneration, burning Fate to survive, initiative replacement and the
    temporary Solo-rank benefit need adjudication. No automatic per-click refresh.
11. After a critical/phenomena ruling, only explicitly recorded state changes are
    applied. An AI description cannot silently add missing mechanical effects.
12. Mission authors choose encounter geometry and allowed interactions. The public
    sample is original, and the private Final Sanction port has its own adaptation
    notes. Source NPC profiles in a quickstart are not full core-created PCs.
